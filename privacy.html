Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Channel Upload Assistant ("the app") is a personal tool operated by a single YouTube creator to manage their own channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses the YouTube API Services to access only the signed-in owner's own YouTube channels: channel details, videos, playlists, captions, thumbnails and YouTube Analytics reports.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, subtitles, playlists) and to read the owner's own performance statistics for content planning. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage, retention and deletion

Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's. The app does not store any other user's data.

Analytics figures read through the YouTube API Services are kept only as local working files and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. The app does not use cookies, does not place tracking technology on any device and does not share data with third parties.

To delete all data held by the app: revoke access (see below) and delete the token and analytics files from the owner's computer. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact below and are completed within 7 days.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access (Google security settings page). Revoking access stops the app immediately; the owner can then delete the locally stored tokens.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/armaanwork484/armaanwork484.github.io/issues or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
